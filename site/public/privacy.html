Privacy Policy

Updated 29 September 2026

This policy covers the Signshot Android app and erashot.app.

Information we collect

None. Signshot has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting. If you email us, we receive your address and message.

On your phone

Photos, drafts, blocks and your terms agreement are stored only on your phone.

Permissions

Camera only.

Publishing

Optional. A published photo is public and permanent on Solana and Arweave: the image, title, text, capture time, wallet address, phone model and, if you hold one, your Seeker Genesis Token.

Third parties

The app connects to your wallet, Solana, Arweave, Google and erashot.app. Each sees your IP address. We sell and share nothing.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Signshot never receives your wallet's private key.

Deletion

Uninstalling deletes what is on your phone. Published photos cannot be deleted.

Children

Not for children under 13.
