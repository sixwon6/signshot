Privacy & publication

Updated 24 September 2026 · Signshot for Android, app.seekershot

Signshot is a camera app that can publish a photograph with a Solana wallet and create a photo NFT. Taking a photo does not publish it. Publication is optional, public and intended to be permanent.

The app has no Signshot account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or automatic crash-reporting service. It connects to your wallet, Solana services and Arweave storage as described below. The website host and those service providers receive normal connection information, including your IP address, and may keep their own operational logs.

What stays on this phone

  • The full-size clean and styled photographs, their local capture records, and any restyled versions.
  • Your selected looks, share-name preference, publication drafts and transaction-recovery information.
  • Your wallet authorization cache in app storage and a separate upload-signing key in secure storage. The upload key is not your wallet's private key. Signshot does not receive your wallet's private key or recovery phrase.
  • The publishers you block and your agreement to the terms. On the collect page they stay in your browser's storage instead. Neither is ever sent to Signshot.

Save photo copies a file to your gallery. Share photo gives a copy to the app you choose. Neither action publishes a new blockchain record. A name stamp, when enabled, changes the shared presentation copy; it does not replace the photograph named in an existing record.

The app requests camera access and no storage permission: saving to the gallery goes through Android's media store. It does not request location or contacts access or browse your photo library. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app. Device-to-device transfer behavior can depend on the Android manufacturer.

When information leaves the phone

Action Information and destination
Connect a wallet Mobile Wallet Adapter sends an authorization request to the wallet app. Signshot receives the public account address and an authorization token.
Read wallet or publication state The wallet address, account addresses and transaction references are sent to the configured Solana RPC provider. This includes checking pending transactions. Genesis Token and .skr name lookups send the wallet address to Solana mainnet, because both exist only there: a build that publishes on devnet asks api.mainnet-beta.solana.com for them. Reads can resume when the app returns to the foreground.
Review publication costs Solana receives account reads and a transaction simulation. The storage service receives the upload-key address and deterministic item identifiers to check capacity. The photograph is not publicly uploaded by this step.
Approve publication Your wallet submits a Solana transaction. It identifies your wallet, the edition, supply, image fingerprint and metadata fingerprint. After confirmation, Signshot uploads the smaller publication image and its metadata to Arweave through ar.io Turbo. If interrupted, the app retains the pending operation for recovery.
Device attestation When you publish, the phone's secure hardware creates a key for that one publication and Android attests it. The public metadata then permanently carries the certificate chain and a signed summary: the phone's brand and model, that it booted verified software with a locked bootloader, its Android version and patch level, this app's package and signing certificate, the photograph's fingerprints and size, and the capture time. It never includes a serial number, IMEI or MEID. Publications made from one phone within a few weeks can share a certificate, so they can be recognised as coming from the same device. If the hardware does not answer, the photograph publishes without this.
Seeker Genesis Token When you publish, Signshot asks Solana mainnet whether your wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token. If it does, the public metadata permanently carries that token's mint address. The address stays the same if the token moves to another of your wallets, so publications from different wallets that name it can be recognised as coming from the same Seeker owner. Anyone viewing the photograph, in the app or on this website, can ask mainnet whether the publishing wallet still holds it. If your wallet holds none, or mainnet does not answer, the photograph publishes without this.
Send an NFT Your wallet signs a transfer identifying the NFT, sender and recipient. The transfer becomes part of Solana's public history. The original publication attribution remains with the photograph.
Set or change a price Your wallet signs a transaction that records the price against the edition in a public Solana account. Only the publisher's wallet can set it.
Collect or buy a copy, in the app or on this website The wallet you choose supplies its public address and signs the transaction. The public edition and claim accounts identify who collected. When a copy has a price, the payment goes to the publisher's address in that same transaction and is public. The app and the page store pending-operation information locally so they can recover after an interruption.
Open the Wallet screen or a collect link The app and the collect page read Signshot's moderation list from erashot.app. The request names no wallet and no edition; the host receives normal connection information.
Report a publication Report opens your email app with a message to support@erashot.app. It names the edition's address and carries its collect link. Nothing is sent until you send it; then we receive the message, anything you add to it, and your email address.

What publication includes

The current print format publishes a compressed image, up to 1600 pixels on its long edge within the upload budget. It does not upload the full-size clean or styled photograph. The public record is the name you give the photograph, the text you put in the Moment field and a link to that image. The Solana transaction carries the edition count, the publisher address and the fingerprints of the image and the record. A publication with device attestation also carries what that row above lists, and one from a wallet that holds a Seeker Genesis Token carries the token's mint address. The look, its strength and your edits are not published. The record contains no automatically collected GPS location or device serial number. Text you type can disclose a place or a date. The app does not verify it as capture evidence.

The time recorded by the blockchain is publication time. A phone-reported capture time is separate. A wallet signature binds publication to the wallet; it does not independently certify the scene, capture device or photographer's identity.

Earlier Memo records remain readable. Those records may include a tiny image in the transaction and camera-result fields in their Arweave metadata, such as exposure, ISO, flash state and a sensor timestamp. New NFT publication does not rewrite these historical records.

Creating an album publishes its name and controlling wallet through a separate Solana transaction. Choosing it when publishing a photograph makes that album association public. Each photograph remains its own edition. The app cannot move a published photograph into another album, remove a member or rename an album.

Providers

  • Solana: the configured RPC service, the wallet's own transaction service and public blockchain network. The app defaults to the public Solana endpoint unless a deployment configures another HTTPS endpoint. Development builds may use a development provider.
  • Arweave and ar.io Turbo: uploads at upload.ardrive.io, upload allowance at payment.ardrive.io, and reads through turbo-gateway.com and arweave.net. The uploaded files are public and may be served by other Arweave gateways.
  • This website: static pages and a read-only Solana proxy hosted with Cloudflare Pages. The proxy sends permitted RPC requests to its configured Solana provider. A collection link that carries &cluster=devnet does not use the proxy: your browser reads the test network directly from api.devnet.solana.com, which then sees your IP address alongside the edition you are looking at. The host and providers may process IP addresses and request logs for delivery and abuse prevention.

Approval, permanence and recovery

Reviewing a quote does not publish the photo. Cancelling before a wallet transaction is submitted leaves a local draft. Once a transaction has been submitted it may still confirm even if the app is closed. After confirmation, upload recovery completes the publication you approved; it does not ask for another NFT-creation transaction.

Once uploaded, the public image and metadata cannot be withdrawn through Signshot. Confirmed blockchain records also cannot be removed through the app. A failed or unavailable gateway does not mean the publication has been deleted. Only publish photographs and details you intend to make public.

Deletion and wallet disconnection

Delete in Photos removes the local capture and associated local files when no unresolved publication needs them. The app prevents deletion when doing so would interrupt a pending transaction or its upload recovery. Removing local files does not remove public records, public images or NFTs held by wallets. Uninstalling the app removes its private files and can interrupt recovery; gallery copies and public data remain.

Clearing Signshot's storage from your phone's app settings removes more than photographs: it deletes the app's Arweave upload key and every publication draft. Neither is included in an Android backup or a device transfer, and a publication that is already recorded on Solana but whose image upload has not finished cannot be completed after that — by this app on this phone or any other. Export the publication bundle from the photograph first if you need to keep it.

Disconnect in Settings revokes the current wallet authorization when the wallet can be reached and clears the app's local authorization. You can also revoke Signshot inside your wallet. Disconnecting does not remove public transactions or change token ownership.

Checking a photograph on this website

The public photo page retrieves the recorded image and metadata to check their fingerprints and edition information. The earlier-record verifier sends the wallet or transaction reference you enter to Solana RPC providers and retrieves associated Arweave metadata. If you select a local file to check, its fingerprint is calculated in your browser; that file is not uploaded. The pages do not add advertising or analytics trackers.

Children and contact

Signshot is not directed at children under 13. Contact support@erashot.app with privacy questions or requests. We can address data under our control; we cannot remove a public blockchain record or withdraw an Arweave upload.
