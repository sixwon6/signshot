Privacy

Updated 29 September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your photographs, drafts, blocks and agreement to the terms stay on your phone.

Publishing

Publishing is optional. A published photograph is public and permanent on Solana and Arweave, and nobody can delete it. It includes a smaller copy of the image, its title and text, the capture time, your wallet address, the phone's model, Android version and boot state from its hardware attestation (never a serial number or IMEI), and your Seeker Genesis Token's address if your wallet holds one. Photographs published from the same phone can be linked to one another, even across wallets.

Collecting, buying, pricing and sending are Solana transactions. The wallet addresses and amounts are public.

Services

The app connects to your wallet, a Solana RPC provider, ar.io Turbo for Arweave uploads, Google's attestation revocation list, and erashot.app for the moderation list. Each receives your IP address. Report opens an email to us with the edition's address; we receive what you send.

Permissions

Camera only. No location, contacts or photo library access.

Children

Signshot is not for children under 13.
