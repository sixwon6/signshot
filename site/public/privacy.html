Privacy

Updated 29 September 2026

We collect nothing. No account, ads, analytics or crash reports. Your photos, blocks and terms agreement stay on your phone.

Publishing is optional, public and permanent. It puts a smaller copy of the photo, its title and text, the capture time, your wallet address, the phone model from its hardware attestation and your Genesis Token, if you hold one, on Solana and Arweave. Photos from the same phone can be linked.

Your wallet, Solana, Arweave, Google and erashot.app see your IP address.
